Foresight – The Layman’s Oracle

Know what every choice actually does – before you make it. Every talent point, stat, item, and buff, explained in plain terms with your character’s real numbers.

WoW makes you choose constantly and explains almost nothing. Foresight is the missing explanation layer: a display-and-advice addon that reads the game’s own data and tells you what your choices do for your character – built for players who want to understand, and for hardcore players who cannot afford to guess.

🔮 It Reads Your Character Back to You

Log in and Foresight introduces you to your own character: your archetype and strongest lens, your biggest hits, what your talent build actually stacks (and the capstone it pays off into), the hidden synergy your class can exploit, and one coaching nudge – once, politely, and again any time you ask with /foresight insights.

🌳 Talents That Explain Themselves

  • Hover any talent – on the real talent frame or in our planner – and see what it touches and what the next point does to your abilities: “Fireball: ~141 → ~148 per hit (+7)”. Hold Shift for the full breakdown.
  • Chains show where a point really pays out – “Intellect → spell damage via Mental Quickness”.
  • Hidden synergies the guides miss: when one stat quietly scales several lanes for your class (the Intellect-stacked Shaman whose points power melee, casting, and totems at once), Foresight says so.
  • Bleeds count: Rend-style damage shows per-tick and total, ranked among your real hits – not silently dropped because it isn’t one swing.

🗺️ A Planner You Can Use at Level 1

  • Click-to-allocate talent trees at any level – including before the game unlocks talents.
  • Example builds for every spec, generated from the live talent data so they’re always legal and current – hover one and Foresight explains why it works before you load it.
  • Import/export build codes, save named builds, plan for a future level, copy an inspected player – and a live overlay that glows your planned path on the real tree as you spend.
  • Suggest-only, always: Foresight never spends a point for you.

⚖️ Gear Verdicts That Tell the Whole Story

  • One-line upgrade verdicts (“Upgrade for Melee DPS +2.8%”) from a real net-swap model of your character – not static stat weights.
  • Weapon swaps account for the whole configuration: hover a two-hander while wearing a shield and Foresight shows the survivability you’d lose with it – the trade is never hidden behind a damage number.
  • “Best for your spells”: which of YOUR abilities this item lifts the most.
  • A gear check that compares what your gear stacks against what actually moves your build – and says so when they disagree.

📊 The Foresight Matrix

Your character’s engine on one dashboard: honest dimension bars, your class’s Enabler → Engine → Payoff loop – spec-aware, so an Arms warrior sees the bleed engine and a Fury warrior sees Flurry, and hybrid builds are named hybrids (“Arms/Fury hybrid (20/21/0)”) – plus best-next-points for your goal and anonymous crowd standings with a daily featured challenge.

🔍 Honesty Is the Feature

  • Numbers come from the game client’s own data files – never copied from guides.
  • Anything not yet verified on Forever is tagged (p) presumed. Your own talent scans verify rank rules live and flip the tag green: “verified from your scan”.
  • When Foresight can’t model something (a proc rate, a trinket effect), it says so out loud. No invented numbers, ever.

🔒 Privacy, Up Front

  • Foresight never networks on its own. The crowd compare is one /foresight share link that you paste into a browser – nothing leaves your machine until you do.
  • Anonymous by default. The public ladders are strictly opt-in, under a nickname you choose.
  • Realm, account, guild, and real names never leave your machine – enforced by a generated allow-list the addon cannot bypass.
