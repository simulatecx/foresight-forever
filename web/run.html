The World Keeps Score

RUN is a standalone addon for WoW: Forever and Hardcore - a Nemesis system the platform can actually keep. Every creature that fells a player climbs a ten-tier notoriety ladder and earns a deterministic title: the same enemy carries the same name for everyone, forever.

Enemies that grow

Kill counts blend your deaths, deaths heard nearby, and the verified crowd - banners, map pins, and soft-elite treatment for the ones that earn it.

Honest odds

FIGHT / RISKY / FLEE with "~N% to win" under every target. Under 50%, solo, the wager appears: win and it's recorded forever.
