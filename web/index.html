Where the Data Comes From

Every number Foresight shows carries its provenance - nothing is invented, and nothing is called verified until it has been seen in the game itself.

Blizzard's own game data is the source of truth. The talent trees, spells, items, and racials are decoded from the WoW: Forever client's data files (via wago.tools exports) - the same files the game reads. When a new build ships, the data regenerates from it.

Playing verifies. In-game, Foresight reads the real tooltips as you (and everyone running the addon) hover talents - those scans confirm or correct every presumed value. Until a number is confirmed it is honestly marked (p) presumed; once your client shows it, it flips to verified.

The crowd confirms, people review. Community-sourced corrections (contributed scans, patch notes, posts) only reach the shipped data through a verification queue - multiple independent confirmations plus human review. Nothing from outside your game is ever auto-applied.

Credited sources. Per-rank talent text is sourced from talentsforever.com, a fan-made Forever talent calculator read from the beta client's data files - credited in the addon and here. Release notes and hotfixes are tracked against each build.
