Foresight privacy

Plain-language promise: we collect game data to power spec comparisons, and nothing that identifies you. This page is the whole story.

What the addon collects (on your machine)

The Foresight addon journals game data locally to its SavedVariables file - item sightings, your talent build, observed buff values, your computed lens numbers. It never sends anything on its own; it has no network access.

What leaves your machine - only when you choose

Only when you run /foresight share and paste the result here, after ticking consent. That payload is built by an allow-list and contains only:

  • class, spec id, and level
  • your computed lens numbers (spellcasting, melee DPS, etc. - just numbers)
  • your talent build (node ids and ranks)
  • item ids you've seen, with counts
  • observed buff/consumable stat values
  • the names of buffs active on you at submission (record context - "buffed send")
  • a random, non-identifying id so we can count distinct contributors and avoid double-counting re-uploads

What we never collect

Character name, realm, account, guild, zone, IP-linked identity, timestamps, or any free text (your /foresight report notes stay on your machine). The payload is shaped both in the addon and again in this page's rules, so these cannot be sent even by a modified client. (The optional public-ladder handle described below is a nickname you choose - not your character name, realm, or real name.)

If you opt in to the public ladders (off by default)

You can choose to appear by name on the public "champion" ladders. This is off unless you turn it on in-game (/foresight set named on) and set a handle. When on - and only then - your submission also carries:

  • a display handle you choose (a nickname - not your character name, realm, or real name)
  • an optional armory/character link, if you add one

Those, alongside your class, level, the metric value, and your active-buff names, are shown publicly on the ladders. Anonymous contributors are never named and never appear as a champion row - their numbers still feed the aggregate averages. This is fully revocable: turn it off with /foresight set named off and future submissions are anonymous again (already-submitted public entries clear on the next aggregation).

Feedback you send (the "Send Word" form)

If you use the feedback form, the message you type is sent to the Foresight team - plus a contact handle only if you choose to add one, and, if you pasted a share link, a single non-identifying context line (class, level, build). Feedback is write-only: it is never shown publicly and never readable by the site. It is plain text you control, so please don't include anything sensitive.

How it's used

Aggregated only - to show "your spellcasting is around the top N% of your spec at your level" and to map what items exist in the game. Brackets with fewer than a handful of contributors show a count only, never a ranking. Raw contributions are never shown to anyone; only the aggregates are readable.

Keeping it / removing it

Contributions are pseudonymous aggregate-source data. Since no account or identity is attached, there is nothing tied to you to look up. You can stop contributing at any time by simply not sharing; to request removal of a specific contribution, keep the random id shown in your share text and contact us.

This notice updates in the same change as any data-practice change. Last updated with Foresight v0.48.0 (opt-in public-ladder tier, S34c).
