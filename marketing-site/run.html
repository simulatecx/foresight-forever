What RUN builds

The world is already keeping score

A Nemesis system the platform can actually keep - reputation, never mob power. Display and record only; RUN never fights for you.

⚔

Enemies that grow in rank

A 10-tier notoriety ladder from first blood to 100+ player kills. Earned titles, never random - the same creature carries the same name for everyone, forever. Counts blend your deaths + deaths heard nearby + the verified crowd.

⚖

Banners for the ones that earn it

Rares, elites, bosses and quest targets ping as they close in - sound, banner, map pin. Soft elites: a "normal" mob with a body count gets the banner real elites get. Quiet by default, total awareness if you want it.

👂

Hear every fall around you

When a player falls, every RUN user hears it live - and the killer's legend grows on your tooltips. Last words ride the ping. Anonymous payloads, checksums, rate limits - in-game only, never the internet.

🎲

RISK IT - and enter the ledger

Honest odds under your target: FIGHT / RISKY / FLEE with "~N% to win". Under 50%, solo, the wager appears: GIANT SLAYER (<25%) or UNDERDOG (25-50%). Win and it's recorded forever. Die, and you feed its legend.

📜

Most Wanted, kept forever

Every alt feeds one account ledger: what killed us, where, and who fell. Your deadliest species wear their earned titles; group wipes go on the record too - "X has wiped your raid."

🌎

The Deadliest, world-wide

Bring your ledger to the crowd (always manual, always allow-listed) and the world's records grow: the global Most Wanted and Giant Slayers boards live on the armory - every creature wearing the title it earned.
